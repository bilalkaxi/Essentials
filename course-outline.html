01

Nahw I · Grammar

Beginner 4 Weeks

Arabic sentence structure: nouns, verbs, and how words relate to one another in a sentence. This is where you learn to see the logic behind every Qur'anic ayah, not just recognize the words.

Format
Live · Zoom
Weekly Commitment
~2–3 hrs
Prerequisite
None

What You'll Learn

  • Tell nominal sentences (jumlah ismiyyah) apart from verbal sentences (jumlah fi'liyyah)
  • Identify Mubtada & Khabar (subject & predicate) in any sentence
  • Recognize the Fa'il (doer) and Maf'ul Bihi (object) of a verb
  • Understand basic Idafa (possessive) constructions
  • Read short Qur'anic phrases and label each word's grammatical role

Weekly Breakdown

WEEK 1
  • Introduction to Nahw — why grammar unlocks meaning
  • The Noun (Ism) vs. the Verb (Fi'l)
  • Nominal vs. Verbal Sentences
WEEK 2
  • Mubtada & Khabar (Subject & Predicate)
  • Fa'il — The Doer of the Verb
WEEK 3
  • Maf'ul Bihi — The Object
  • Idafa — The Possessive Construction
WEEK 4
  • Harf Jarr — Prepositions
  • Introduction to I'rab (Case Endings)
  • Review & Practice

Prerequisite: None

02

Sarf I · Morphology

Beginner 4 Weeks

Verb roots and conjugation patterns — how a single three-letter root generates an entire family of related words. Once this clicks, Qur'anic vocabulary stops looking like thousands of unrelated words.

Format
Live · Zoom
Weekly Commitment
~2–3 hrs
Prerequisite
None — can be taken alongside Nahw I

What You'll Learn

  • Understand the trilateral root (al-fi'l al-thulathi) system
  • Conjugate Al-Madi (past) and Al-Mudari' (present) for common pronouns
  • Form the Amr (command) from any regular verb
  • Recognize Ism al-Fa'il, Ism al-Maf'ul, and Al-Masdar on sight
  • Spot common Sarf patterns as they appear in Qur'anic verses

Weekly Breakdown

WEEK 1
  • Introduction to Sarf & the Root System
  • The Trilateral Root (Al-Fi'l al-Thulathi)
WEEK 2
  • Al-Madi — The Past Tense
  • Al-Mudari' — The Present Tense
WEEK 3
  • Al-Amr — The Command Form
  • Ism al-Fa'il & Ism al-Maf'ul
WEEK 4
  • Al-Masdar — The Verbal Noun
  • The Ten Verb Forms — Overview
  • Review & Practice

Prerequisite: None — can be taken alongside Nahw I

03

Intermediate Sarf II · Morphology

Intermediate 4 Weeks

Building directly on Sarf I — weak-letter verbs, all ten verb forms in depth, and the irregular patterns that make up a large share of the words actually used throughout the Qur'an.

Format
Live · Zoom
Weekly Commitment
~3–4 hrs
Prerequisite
Sarf I

What You'll Learn

  • Conjugate assimilated (mithal), hollow (ajwaf), and defective (naqis) weak verbs
  • Apply all ten verb forms (I–X) with confidence
  • Recognize hamza-initial and doubled-root (mudaaf) verb patterns
  • Analyze irregular Qur'anic verb forms using root-based reasoning

Weekly Breakdown

WEEK 1
  • Review of the Root System
  • Weak Letters — Assimilated Verbs (Mithal)
WEEK 2
  • Weak Letters — Hollow (Ajwaf) & Defective (Naqis) Verbs
WEEK 3
  • The Ten Verb Forms In-Depth (Forms I–V)
WEEK 4
  • The Ten Verb Forms In-Depth (Forms VI–X)
  • Review & Practice

Prerequisite: Sarf I

04

Intermediate Nahw II · Grammar

Intermediate 4 Weeks

A deeper pass through Arabic grammar — full case-ending (I'rab) rules, conditional and exceptive particles, and parsing complete, multi-clause Qur'anic ayat rather than isolated phrases.

Format
Live · Zoom
Weekly Commitment
~3–4 hrs
Prerequisite
Nahw I

What You'll Learn

  • Apply full I'rab (nominative, accusative, genitive) across sentence types
  • Recognize conditional sentences (shart & jawab al-shart)
  • Identify exceptive particles (istithna) and emphasis constructions (tawkid)
  • Parse complete, multi-clause Qur'anic ayat grammatically

Weekly Breakdown

WEEK 1
  • Review of Nahw I
  • Full I'rab — Nominative, Accusative & Genitive
WEEK 2
  • Conditional Sentences (Shart & Jawab al-Shart)
WEEK 3
  • Exceptive Particles (Istithna)
  • Emphasis Constructions (Tawkid)
WEEK 4
  • Parsing Full Ayat
  • Review & Practice

Prerequisite: Nahw I

05

Balagha · Rhetoric

Advanced 4 Weeks

The science of Arabic eloquence — how the Qur'an's precise word choice, sentence structure, and imagery achieve their rhetorical power. The capstone course, tying Nahw and Sarf into literary appreciation.

Format
Live · Zoom
Weekly Commitment
~3–4 hrs
Prerequisite
Nahw II & Sarf II (or instructor approval)

What You'll Learn

  • Understand the three branches of Balagha: Ma'ani, Bayan, and Badi'
  • Identify simile (tashbih) and metaphor (isti'ara) in a text
  • Recognize metonymy (kinaya) and word-order emphasis techniques
  • Appreciate select examples of Qur'anic eloquence in their full depth

Weekly Breakdown

WEEK 1
  • Introduction to Balagha
  • Ilm al-Ma'ani — Sentence Purpose & Emphasis
WEEK 2
  • Ilm al-Bayan — Tashbih (Simile) & Isti'ara (Metaphor)
WEEK 3
  • Ilm al-Bayan — Kinaya (Metonymy)
  • Ilm al-Badi' — Overview
WEEK 4
  • Balagha in Qur'anic Ayat
  • Review & Practice

Prerequisite: Nahw II & Sarf II (or instructor approval)
