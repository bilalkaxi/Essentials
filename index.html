Your Instructor

Bilal Ibn Jamil

Lead Instructor, Essential Qur'anic Arabic

I founded Essential Qur'anic Arabic out of a deep love for the Qur'an and a desire to connect people to their Creator. I studied under Bayyinah Institute's Dream Intensive program and am currently completing a degree in Computer Science. Over the past 2+ years, I've taught Nahw, Sarf, and Tajweed to over 10 students across 3 batches. Developed my own curriculum and teaching method, all inspired by the Qur'an itself.

My students don't just learn grammar; they learn to understand the Qur'an within a year, experiencing its words in salah and from the mushaf. It's never been about credentials — it's about connection.

Questions

Before you register.

Any demo class?

1st Session will be available for everybody to join, if you want to see if it's the right fit for you. Charges will only apply afterward, if the student wants to continue further.

Do I need any prior knowledge of Arabic?

You just need to be able to read arabic.

What if I miss a live class?

Every session is recorded and shared in the batch WhatsApp group within 24 hours, so you never fall behind because of a missed class.

Is this batch open to sisters?

This batch is brothers only. Sister's batch availability will depends on quantity of sisters applied

How much time should I set aside each week?

Plan for one live class plus roughly 1–2 hours of homework and review per week — more if you want to move faster than the pace of the group.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes — a certificate for each of the 5 courses you complete, plus a separate Program Certificate once all five are finished.

What platform are classes held on?

Live, interactive classes on Zoom at 10:00 PM (After Isha, Pakistan Standard Time). A link is shared in the WhatsApp group before each session.
